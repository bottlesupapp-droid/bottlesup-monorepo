- [ ] **Deposit wording: CHECKED 3 Nov, it does NOT match; fix waits for your decision.** The server splits a combination deposit into a reservation fee, a credit toward the minimum spend and admission, and only the credit share counts toward the bottle bill (`depositAllocation.ts`). The app never reads the terms: it knows only the on/off switch `deposit_is_credit`. So for a venue with a combination, (1) the note on the table booking screen (`min-spend-note`) says the whole deposit "counts toward" the minimum spend and how much is "left to spend at the venue", too little; (2) the "due at the venue" amount in the booking quote (`quoteTable`, used by the table booking screen) treats the whole table deposit as credit, so for a combination it is understated; (3) nothing tells the customer about the fee or admission share. The amount actually charged is right (the server works it out). Not looked into: the add-bottles screen passes `depositIsCredit: false` (no credit at all), which is a separate question. I cannot tell from here whether any venue uses a combination yet (production was not read); it went live 2 Nov. Why it is not just fixed: the venue's raw terms also hold BottlesUp's revenue split (`venue_pct`), which should not go to every customer, so the choice is how to show it. Options: (a) the website returns the credit share and the app words it (a website function plus app change); (b) the app reads the booking's own terms snapshot after booking only; (c) for now the app says "part of your deposit may count" for any venue whose terms are not a single purpose. Say which and I build it.
